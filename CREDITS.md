# Credits

Everything below is free to use in a game that's sold. The sounds and music are **CC0 (public domain)** from
[OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org). Credit isn't required for CC0, but these people deserve it.
`tools/sfx/import_real_sounds.py` downloads and prepares them.
