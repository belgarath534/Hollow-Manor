Hollow Manor, first playable. Use headphones.

Find the front door key (it's in a different drawer or cupboard every game), then unlock the front door in the foyer without getting caught. Listen for the Hunter's boots and voice, and hide when he's close.

Touch: swipe up to walk (swipe up again to run, tap to stop), swipe left or right to turn, double tap to search or hide, two-finger tap to describe the room, three-finger tap to listen for the Hunter.
